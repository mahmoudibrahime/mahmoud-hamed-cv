About

Careful dispensing. Clear advice. Reliable service.

I graduated from the Faculty of Pharmacy, Tanta University in 2016 with a Very Good grade point average. Since then I have worked in a medical centre environment — including during my military service — and in community pharmacy, where most of my daily work is face to face with patients.

My approach is simple: dispense accurately, explain clearly, and never guess. When a case needs a physician, I refer. I completed an accredited Over-the-Counter (OTC) course at the Faculty of Medicine, Menoufia University, and clinical pharmacy coursework at Tanta University, so I am comfortable advising on minor ailments while recognising the limits of self-care.

I am now seeking a community pharmacist position in Saudi Arabia with Al Nahdi Medical Company or Al Dawaa Pharmacies, where I can combine patient counselling with strong inventory discipline and teamwork.

  • Accurate prescription dispensing and medication safety
  • OTC recommendations with appropriate referral
  • Patient counselling in Arabic and English
  • Inventory, stock rotation and expiry awareness
